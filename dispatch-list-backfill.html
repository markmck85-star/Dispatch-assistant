Load old dispatch lists

Adds past Neumo dispatch lists to the history behind the "Calls at" columns on the Restock Forecast. It does not touch the board, tickets or texts.

  1. Choose the saved list files (.eml). Select them all at once.
  2. Check the summary, then press Load.
