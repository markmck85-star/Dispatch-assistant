SMS Text Alerts & Consent

What this program is

Internal Field Dispatch Alerts

MCR Technical Service uses SMS text messages to notify our own technicians and dispatch staff when a new service ticket, trouble call, or scheduled restock comes in that requires their attention. This program is for MCR's internal field service team only — it is not a marketing or promotional messaging program, and it is not directed at MCR's customers or the general public.

A typical message looks like:

“MCR Dispatch: New trouble ticket — Site: Hall County Kroger Jesse Jewell, WO#00151697, Issue: Journal Printer. Reply for details.”

How to opt in

Who Receives These Messages

Enrollment is limited to current MCR technicians and dispatchers who choose to receive these alerts as part of coordinating field service work. Opting in is entirely optional and is never a condition of employment or assignment.

To be added to (or removed from) the alert list, an MCR staff member can contact their supervisor or MCR management directly at the contact information below.

Message details

Frequency, Rates & Opt-Out

  • Message frequency varies based on field service ticket volume — typically a handful of messages per day per recipient, more on busy days.
  • Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan.
  • To stop receiving messages at any time, reply STOP to any message, or contact MCR management directly to be removed from the list.
  • For help, reply HELP to any message, or use the contact information below.
Privacy

How We Use Your Information

Phone numbers collected for this program are used solely to deliver field dispatch alerts to MCR's own staff. We do not sell, rent, or share phone numbers or SMS opt-in data with third parties for their own marketing purposes. Standard message and data rates from your carrier may apply.
