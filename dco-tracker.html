Counter and testing sites are marked not applicable automatically, since their forms are changed by the state's own staff; use Include to bring one back. A site counts as done when its change-out ticket was completed with a visit, when a closing note on or after the start date says the change-out was performed, or when someone marks it here. A note that mentions a change-out but reads as not done is listed under "Needs a look". Each site is tracked once; individual printers are not tracked separately.
