Estimates are good to about a day. Friday and holiday-weekend restocks tend to leave more forms behind, so those sites may run a little higher than shown. Florida and North Carolina are left out because their counts are only percentages.

"Calls at" is the typical form count a site had when it was put on the dispatch list, by the day the list was sent (the number in brackets is how many lists it is based on). Treat any figure based on fewer than three lists as a rough guide. "Neumo list" compares the estimate with that weekday's figure: "likely listed" means it is already at or under it, "next day" means one more day of use gets it there. It only shows when there are at least three past lists for that weekday, and not on weekends.
