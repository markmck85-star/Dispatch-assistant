Mileage check

Unidentified stops

Place names on the mileage sheets that the system could not match to a known site. Tell it once what a name means and that technician's later sheets recognize it (saved names apply to that technician only). Suggestions are ranked by how many of that name's hops the miles fit (all saved sheets in the window are pooled), and a green confident tag means only one site fits every hop; a star means the site was on the dispatch list or had a ticket that day or the day before. A name that is a technician's own home can be marked as home. Names are shown per technician, most repeated first.
